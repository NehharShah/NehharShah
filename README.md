# Nihar Shah

**I build software that acts.**

I'm a systems engineer and researcher working across AI infrastructure, backend systems, developer tooling, and autonomous software.

At **Ostium**, I build low-latency infrastructure for automated hedging, risk, execution, market connectivity, and production reliability. I also built the internal agent platform used for engineering and operational workflows.

Previously at **Subconscious AI, StarkWare, and Nethermind**, I worked on research agents, model infrastructure, distributed systems, and verification.

I'm interested in the point where software stops producing answers and starts taking actions: understanding state, making decisions, interacting with real systems, and remaining observable, constrained, and recoverable.

## Research

I research what changes when software can take consequential actions under uncertainty.

My work spans **agent oversight, reliability and control, verification, mechanism design, market structure, and risk**.

Recent work includes autonomous market making, liquidation and solvency, funding mechanisms, settlement verification, and tail-risk control for side-effecting agents.

My paper, **Calibrated agents as market makers: automated liquidity provision for long-tail prediction markets**, was recently accepted for publication in Digital Finance (Springer). The work establishes optimal market-making spread decompositions based on agent estimation error to solve liquidity constraints in sparse or uncertain environments.
